This is Bottle Harmony: a water sort puzzle game with beautiful glass, natural pouring sounds and room to think.

A simple touch. A thoughtful challenge.
Tap a glass, then choose another to pour. Bring matching colors together and work towards a neatly sorted board. The idea is easy to grasp; the pleasure is in seeing a plan come together.

Start with a two-color introduction and follow a mainline of 1,000 puzzles, unlocking them in order. As more colors enter the mix, each empty space becomes a possibility. Take a quick puzzle break or settle in for a longer session—there is no countdown to hurry you along.

Make the glass your own
Choose freely from 15 glass container styles, including bottles, test tubes, tumblers and stemware. Swipe on the home screen to find a favorite. The whole board follows your choice, so a familiar puzzle can feel fresh in a different glass.

Keep the momentum
Undo a pour or restart a level for free. Every new mainline level offers one free one-step hint; more hints use credits earned by completing levels. Revisit completed puzzles whenever you want, with free replay hints.

A little twist beyond the mainline
After each group of 20 mainline puzzles, a bonus puzzle becomes available. Explore 50 bonus puzzles with a frozen bottom layer that you can melt for free.

Color you can see. Water you can hear.
Enjoy the glass details, recorded pouring sounds and a firework celebration when a board is complete. Prefer quiet? Switch the sound off. Optional color-linked symbols give you another way to distinguish the colors.

Just you and the puzzle
No advertising. No in-app purchases. No account to create, lives to refill or timer to race. Gameplay works offline, and your progress and preferences stay on your device.
